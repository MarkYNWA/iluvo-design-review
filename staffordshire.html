Prepare a business enquiry

Contact the right
planning authority.

Staffordshire County Council directs housing and most commercial planning enquiries to the relevant district or borough council, or Stoke-on-Trent City Council. The county council handles a different planning remit.

Use the council directory or postcode lookup to find the appropriate authority for your site. For multiple properties, record each address separately; the same authority may not cover every location.

Useful details for the brief.

Should I include Stoke-on-Trent as Staffordshire?

Give the actual town and postcode. Stoke-on-Trent has its own city council; the linked official directory helps you identify the right planning contact.

Can I enquire for a rented or leased property?

Explain your role and who owns the property. Any permissions and contracting arrangements need to be clarified before work can proceed.

Can one enquiry cover several sites?

Yes. Use the business route, give the number of sites and describe the scope. Availability and requirements must be established for each location.
