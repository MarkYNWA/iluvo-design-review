A considered system.
From the beginning.

Roof size is only part of the picture. Your electricity use, when you are at home, shading and future plans all help shape a suitable design.

ILUVO’s role covers technical surveys, system design and installation coordination. Installation and certification are carried out by the appointed delivery partners.

01 / UNDERSTAND

Start with the property.

Discuss your electricity use, roof layout, ownership and any existing equipment. Identify what needs checking during a survey.

02 / DESIGN

Compare your options.

Consider the proposed panel layout, equipment and whether battery storage fits your needs. Ask for clear assumptions behind any performance estimate.

03 / COORDINATE

Know who does what.

Confirm the delivery partner, scope, costs, approvals, certification and handover arrangements before agreeing to proceed.

Before you decide.

What information helps at the first conversation?

Your postcode, property type, a recent electricity bill or annual usage figure, and anything you know about your roof or existing solar. You do not need to upload bills for an initial enquiry.

Will you guarantee a saving?

No. A proposal should explain its assumptions and limitations. Property conditions, system design, electricity use and tariffs all affect the outcome.

Can I explore finance?

Our finance guide explains the government scheme. ILUVO’s own lender arrangements are not confirmed.
