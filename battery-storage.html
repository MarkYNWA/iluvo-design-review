The right questions.
Before the equipment.

How much electricity do you use, and when? What solar and inverter equipment do you already have? Where could a battery be safely located?

Those questions help establish the scope of a survey. Compatibility, capacity and installation requirements need checking before a system is recommended.

01 / NEW SOLAR

Plan the whole system.

Consider panels and storage together, with a design that reflects the property and your daily routine.

02 / EXISTING SOLAR

Check what is already there.

Share the make and model of your inverter and any system paperwork. The survey can establish which upgrade routes are suitable.

03 / YOUR PRIORITIES

Be clear about expectations.

Discuss your goals, available space, budget and future plans. Ask what the proposed system includes and what it does not.

Useful questions to ask.

Can every existing solar system accept a battery?

Do not assume compatibility. Existing equipment, wiring and the proposed battery need a technical assessment.

Will a battery provide power during a cut?

Do not assume backup is included. Ask the designer to confirm whether backup is supported, what additional equipment is required and which circuits would be covered.

What should I have ready?

Your postcode, annual electricity use if known, and details of any existing panels, inverter and battery. A first conversation can establish what else is needed.
