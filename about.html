Meet our team.

As a family-run business, we value personal service, clear communication and accountability. Our team brings experience in renewables, domestic energy assessment, retrofit assessment and project coordination.

Qualifications within our team include a Level 5 Diploma in Retrofit Coordination and Risk Management. Our background includes support for government-backed retrofit projects, alongside renewables consultancy and practical project coordination.

ILUVO supports enquiries, technical surveys, system design and installation coordination. Installation and certification are carried out by appointed delivery partners.

Your written proposal should identify the contracting party, installing partner, relevant certification, warranties and support arrangements. A partner’s credentials should be attributed to that partner.
