PROJECT GUIDE / BEFORE YOU DECIDE

A clearer quote.
A better informed decision.

Use these questions to compare proposals on the same basis. A headline price is only one part of the project.

Explore the details ↓
01

What exactly is included?

Ask for the panel quantity, proposed layout, inverter and battery models, survey scope, installation work and handover documentation. Check scaffolding, access work and any exclusions or allowances.

02

What is behind the estimate?

Ask which roof conditions, shading, electricity use and tariff assumptions were used. Keep estimates separate from guarantees and compare proposals using consistent assumptions.

03

What does the battery do?

Ask about usable capacity, compatibility, operating settings and whether backup is included. Request a clear explanation of any additional equipment and limitations.

04

Who is responsible for the work?

Identify the business you contract with, who installs and certifies the system, who checks permissions and connection requirements, and who resolves issues after handover.

05

What happens after installation?

Request the warranty terms, monitoring and handover arrangements, support contact and process for reporting faults. Distinguish product cover from workmanship cover.

06

What will you pay and when?

Check the complete price, payment stages, exclusions and any conditions. If considering finance, compare the lender’s approved terms and total amount repayable. ILUVO’s finance arrangements are not yet confirmed.
